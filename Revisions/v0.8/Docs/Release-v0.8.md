# v0.8 playtest revision

This separate revision preserves the v0.7 playtest. All players in a multiplayer match must run v0.8. Native, rendered-input and four-process network checks passed; see [validation](Validation-v0.8.md).

- **House of Laban:** the Age-II Winery trains Concubines and produces Drunkenness; Winery Abundance adds 3 Food/s in Age III. The Great and Spacious Building unlocks in Age IV, is limited to one, produces five times Winery Drunkenness, and projects the Concubine aura. Concubines boost nearby laborers by 20% and display their aura. Chief Captains and King's Chariots unlock in III; One of the Fifty in IV.
- **Economy and raids:** trader routes can reach waypoints on occupied ground. Storehouses can set newborn robbers' raid routes. Robbers take loaded laborer cargo and return it for delivery. Hideouts accept Gadianton foot units up to five population. Lawyer conversion is labeled Bribe and reveals the Lawyer while channeling and briefly afterward.
- **Presentation:** Roman numerals show locked ages; the minimap rotates with the camera and retains accurate clicks. Eight new Laban building images have distinct silhouettes. Seats are larger, homes smaller, and the Great and Spacious Building largest. Every unit and building has a finite death or collapse animation. Robber selection includes “I loooooove precious things” with a replaceable synthesized voice clip.
- **Recovered design rules:** nonlinear Nephite Favor HP, 60-second hostility before conversion and capacity checks throughout; Gadianton ordinary gathering at 35% and concealed headquarters/camps; beast-tier foundations with cross-tier completed-building repair; Laban gates; Priestcrafter Temple occupancy and outside aura; Teancum's Night Raid button.

The large Nephite HP curve is intentional restoration of the vault: Favor 0 gives 1 HP, 50 gives base HP, 75 gives 10×, and 100 gives 100×. This materially changes balance from v0.7.
