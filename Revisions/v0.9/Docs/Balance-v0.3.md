# v0.3 balance and scripture notes`r`n`r`n> Historical v0.3 specification. Several mechanics were replaced in v0.4, including civic decay, trader rewards, independent Jaredite livestock, and the age-choice matrix. Use [Balance v0.4](Balance-v0.4.md) for current content.

This file records the v0.3 runtime values and separates them from the scripture passages that inspired names and settings. Scripture does not establish the numerical balance values below.

## Runtime values

- Existing moving units use 1.25× their former speed. Food gathering uses 1.5× its former base rate. Hill of Ore increases nearby ore gathering by 50%.
- Each player's civic resource starts at 20. Its decay per second is `0.003 + 0.00005 × max(0, stock − 20)^2`. A successful conversion awards 5 to the converter and removes 5 from the previous owner. A Nephite direct kill of a civilian removes 8 Favor. Lamanites gain 3 civic stock for a lost unit and 5 for a lost building.
- A completed Temple of the Land generates 0.1 civic resource per second. Each priest or lawyer religious unit generates 0.06 per second. A completed, unscouted Secret Combination Lodge generates 0.16 Lasciviousness per second; discovery stops its production. After Order of the Nehors, each Priestcrafter also generates 0.25 Precious Things per second.
- Civic modifiers use the player's current stock: Nephite Favor increases unit HP by 0.4% per point; Lamanite Anger increases gathering and attack rate by 0.3% per point; Gadianton Lasciviousness increases plunder and siphon by 0.6% per point; Jaredite Dominion increases unit HP by 0.3% and damage by 0.25% per point. For House of Laban, One of the Fifty splash damage is `0.35 × base damage × Favor / 100`; radius is `1.4m + 0.015m × Favor`.
- Religious units other than Nephite Teachers may convert from Age II. Nephite Teachers need Mighty Change of Heart. Religious-unit conversion channels for 12 seconds. The Lamanite Rameumptom has a separate 18-second conversion channel. Ammonites retain their separate “Prostrate before the earth” stance: 12 seconds active, 60-second cooldown.
- Sheep generate 0.3 Food per second. Cumeloms, Cureloms, and Elephants heal 4 HP per second while within 8m beyond the radius of a friendly completed Gathering of Flocks; healing does not stack. Jaredite Cumeloms are available from the Seat of the People without Curelom Husbandry; that research only unlocks individual evolution into Curelom.
- Each completed Trader visit to an allied laborer grants 2 Precious Things, with a minimum 3-second interval between visits. Lawyers can compromise an enemy Seat of the People or Storehouse: after a 3-second channel, they transfer 0.4 of each available resource per second, multiplied by the Gadianton Plunder multiplier.
- Hero age choices grant one passive each: +10% faction military damage or +10% worker gathering. Every faction has one choice of each type at both Age II and Age III. The choices select a hero; the heroes do not receive extra active abilities from this system. Tubaloth has no special active Muster ability.

These values are current implementation values, not balance recommendations. `catalog.json` repeats the machine-readable values under `balance_v03` where appropriate; definition stats and behavior text remain on their individual definitions.

## Scripture inspiration and limits

- Ether describes Jaredite armies and people going to battle with weapons, shields, breastplates, and head-plates. This supports a shielded or armored visual archetype broadly; the catalog’s differentiated Jaredite roles and exact stats are game design. [Ether 15:15](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/ether/15?lang=eng) and [Ether 14:1–2](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/ether/14?lang=eng).
- The record names rival Jaredite rulers and commanders including Coriantumr, Shiz, Lib, Gilead, and Shared. It does not sort these figures into the two game factions or define their abilities; roster assignment and passive perks are design decisions. [Ether 14](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/ether/14?lang=eng), [Ether 15](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/ether/15?lang=eng), [Ether 13:23–24](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/ether/13?lang=eng).
- The account describes Akish’s secret society and the resulting political violence; the lodge and its hidden resource generation are invented mechanics. [Ether 8:13–26](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/ether/8?lang=eng), [Ether 9:1–12](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/ether/9?lang=eng).
- Tubaloth is identified as Ammoron’s son and Lamanite king, and he appoints Coriantumr to lead an army. The passage gives no special ability; Tubaloth is an Age-III hero choice with the same military or economy passive options as the other heroes. [Helaman 1:15–17](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/hel/1?lang=eng).
- Laman and Lemuel are described as murmuring and resisting their father in 1 Nephi 2; the duo hero is a requested fictional game abstraction, not a depiction of a specific battle. [1 Nephi 2:11–16](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/1-ne/2?lang=eng).
- The Rameumptom and Zoramite worship are described in Alma 31. A tower that converts military units is not in the text; it is game design. [Alma 31](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/31?lang=eng).
- Nehor introduces priestcraft, while later passages describe lawyers and corrupt judges among the Gadianton network. Recruitable religious units and their passive resource generation are game mechanics. [Alma 1:3–16](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/1?lang=eng), [Helaman 6:38–39](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/hel/6?lang=eng), [3 Nephi 6:21–30](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/3-ne/6?lang=eng).
