Preserve six factions: Nephites, Lamanites, Gadianton Robbers, Coriantumr Jaredites, Shiz Jaredites and House of Laban. Three spendable resources: Food, Timber, Wealth. Three ages: Settlement, Fortification, Mobilization. Population cap remains 200 per player. Victory concerns enemy headquarters and the specified replacement timer, or resignation; no record-preservation victory.

Jobs preserve the worker; beast evolution preserves the individual; Ammonites are Nephite conversion-only noncombatants. Preserve Favor endpoints (1 maximum HP at zero Favor; 100 times post-upgrade base HP at full Favor), exact faction whitelists, conserved plunder and private local Liahona draft checks. Do not add a seventh faction, fourth resource, campaign roster, general diplomacy or opponent AI to the release requirements.
