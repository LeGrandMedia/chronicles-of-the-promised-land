# Chronicles of the Promised Land v0.7

Version 0.7 brings the 49 requested playtest changes to the game. Native, rendered-input, and four-process network checks passed; see [validation](Validation-v0.7.md).

Run the revision from PowerShell:

```powershell
cd "D:\book of mormon RTS\Revisions\v0.7"
.\Play.cmd
```

Religious play now uses five-second conversion channels without a conversion cooldown, and converted units cannot be converted again. Religious units auto-target, and right-click issues a conversion command. Nephite Teachers can convert without research, Ammonites gain Prostrate after Mighty Change of Heart, and Jaredite religious units can convert. Lamanite conversions create Zoramite Captains, who generate Anger and bolster nearby troops; rising Anger also speeds Lamanite training. Conversion rewards, Nephite Favor losses, Lamanite Anger research, King Noah’s generation bonus, and the expanded Rameumptom effects are included. Nephites and Lamanites have three distinct hero choices at each of Ages II–IV.

The civilizations have revised economies and rosters. Lamanite workers are called Idolatrous Laborers, and their roster has no Watchtower. Gadianton workers can gather Timber and Precious Things; Bands of Kishkumen can raid and Gadianton Robbers can garrison in Hideouts. The Winery produces Food and can research more Food and Drunkenness. Drunken Concubines improve nearby workers’ gathering and construction. Jaredite beast-income research and age-choice bonuses, the flock limit upgrade, Age-III Cumeloms and Age-IV Elephants are included. Elephants produce four times as much Cement and are limited to one per flock. Glowing Stones now increase Jaredite building health. Cement Towers use a long-range, splash-damage trebuchet attack.

Maps, building, and match setup have changed: explored landmarks remain visible outside line of sight, foundations can be placed outside vision, queued structures and full wall lines show foundations, wall segments can become gates, and Jaredite animals can build multiple segments. The lobby offers starting resources, game speed, land fruitfulness, and fog choices. Building repair by right-click, economic rally defaults, task pings that snap to their target, double-tap control-group camera snapping, player-colored range rings, and selected-unit area, multiplier, and trickle readouts improve feedback and control. Trickle notifications appear after each ten resources, and tooltip boxes fit their text. Workers no longer use military auto-attack behavior, and Jaredite animals are excluded from idle-worker detection. Military units unlock by age: weak units in Age II, most in Age III, and the remaining units in Age IV. Sorry messages now acknowledge repentance, and the Liahona popup appears only after it has been broken.

Units are smaller, and unfinished buildings use a distinct foundation appearance. Map units use procedural animation of 2D illustrations for movement, combat, gathering, building, and other actions; they are not rigged 3D characters. The revision includes 13 new images and 238 unique PNG assets in total. See the [art gallery](ArtGallery.html). Earlier revision builds remain preserved.

The [revision checklist](RevisionTasks-v0.7.md) tracks all requested changes and their best-suited model labels.
