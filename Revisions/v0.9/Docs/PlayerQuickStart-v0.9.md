# Playing v0.9

Windows: run the installer, or extract the entire portable ZIP and open `Chronicles.exe`. Keep its `Chronicles` and `Engine` directories beside it. The installer includes the runtime prerequisite package; players do not need Unreal Editor or developer tools.

For solo play, create a hosted lobby and add bots to open slots. Pick a civilization and map, then start. Begin by selecting laborers and right-clicking berries, wild game, trees or ore. Spend Food to train more workers and build an economy before expanding your army.

For LAN play, every player uses this same handoff build and joins the same local network. One player hosts; others enter the host's LAN IPv4 address. Hosting uses UDP 7777. Windows may ask to allow the game on the Private network. Guest Wi-Fi isolation can prevent clients from finding each other. Internet matchmaking is not included.

- Left-click selects. Drag a box to select several units; Shift adds to selection.
- Right-click gives the selected units a contextual order: move, gather, attack, build, repair or enter a carrier where eligible.
- With a laborer selected, Q opens the build menu. The action cards show their keyboard shortcuts.
- Period selects an idle laborer; Ctrl+Period selects all idle laborers. The idle counter is clickable.
- Training buildings accept rally points. The Seat has separate economic and military rally controls.
- F1 opens help. Hotkeys can be changed in the controls menu.
- The minimap rotates with the camera. Click it to move the view.

All nine requested locations are included: Bountiful, Moronihah, Ammonihah, the Land Northward, the Land Southward, the Narrow Neck of Land, Middoni, the Hill Cumorah, and the Waters of Mormon. Extras include Ripliancum, the Valley of Lemuel and the Great and Spacious Courtyard, alongside the original three maps.

This is a development handoff. Balance needs more playtesting, especially Gadianton. Large units may visually clip existing gates despite their navigation passing. LAN has been tested with separate processes on one Windows computer; physical cross-device and Android/Windows LAN remain unverified.
