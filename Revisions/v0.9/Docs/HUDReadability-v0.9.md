# Match HUD readability in v0.9

GPT-6.2 Sol was recommended and used for the HUD layout and readability changes.

The earlier Windows captures showed tiny resource labels and order keys, abbreviated order names, and a Liahona title extending into the bottom edge. The 888 × 500 input capture showed the same issues at a smaller physical size. [Earlier Gadianton scene](Evidence/v0.9/maps/opening-raids-final/scene-1600x900/scene3d-gpu-gadianton.png) · [Earlier compact input capture](Evidence/v0.9/maps/opening-raids-final/input-888x500/input-gameplay.png)

The top bar gives resource amounts and names separate rows. A count appears beside each resource with assigned workers, and selecting the resource card cycles through them. Help displays both its label and F1 on desktop. Resource and command text use a minimum physical size on compact viewports. Card labels fit their available widths. Tooltips provide full production names and keyboard shortcuts. [Top bar](../Game/Source/Chronicles/RTSHUD.cpp) · [Command and production cards](../Game/Source/Chronicles/RTSSelectionHUD.cpp)

Command cards size themselves to their captions and keys. Production and construction cards reserve two lines for names and use separate space for shortcuts. The Liahona has an inset footer with room for its title or the chat recovery instruction. Minimap drawing, camera rotation, and clicks use the same square bounds. Wall placement instructions stay inside the playable viewport while the pointer approaches an edge. [Minimap](../Game/Source/Chronicles/RTSMinimap.cpp) · [Shortcut settings](../Game/Source/Chronicles/RTSHotkeyHUD.cpp) · [Route and wall prompts](../Game/Source/Chronicles/RTSRouteHUD.cpp)
