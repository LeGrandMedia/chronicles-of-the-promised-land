# Source Ledger

Mechanics, prices, cooldowns, faction rosters and dialogue are game adaptations. References identify inspiration, not proof of invented abilities or verbatim quotations. Exact voice lines/event references live in [[Voice Lines]].

## Interpretation boundaries

- Alma24: adult Ammonite nonviolence inspires elite healer/workers. Their sons in Alma56–57 are separate combatants.
- Ether9:19 names elephants, cureloms/cumoms; it does not specify anatomy, evolution or builder roles. Retain the user's game spelling **Cumelom**.
- Helaman3 cement imagery does not establish Jaredite exclusivity; that reassignment is intentional game fiction.
- Alma18 chariots establish royal travel, not a documented battle-chariot army. House of Laban as a civilization, professional infiltrators, exaggerated Favor, the profanity-sensitive Liahona and Shiz's final-strike power are adaptations.
- Three Nephites help/cameos may use folklore as well as 3Nephi28; do not attribute roadside-helper details to that chapter.
- No new comic dialogue is labeled a direct quotation; no modern organizations or skin-color mechanics.
