# v0.9 retained civilization rules

This note records the civilization rules currently retained in `Revisions/v0.9` source. It supplements the v0.8 canonical baseline; it does not rewrite the v0.8 design history, validation, or source exports. For evidence and the current review boundary, see [v0.9 civilization balance](../../Revisions/v0.9/Docs/CivilizationBalance-v0.9.md) and [v0.9 development status](../../Revisions/v0.9/Docs/Development-v0.9.md).

| Rule | Retained v0.9 behavior |
|---|---|
| Nephite Favor and HP | At Favor 0–50, set `t = Favor / 50` and compute max HP as `round(1 + (B − 1) × t × (1.4 − 0.4t))`, where `B` is post-upgrade base HP. Above 50, max HP rises linearly from `B` at Favor 50 to `3B` at Favor 100. Preserve current health fraction. Thus Favor 0/50/75/100 yields about 1 HP / base HP / 2× base HP / 3× base HP. |
| Nephite recruitment | Ordinary units cost 1.25× and take 1.20× listed time. |
| Lamanite recruitment | Ordinary units cost 75% and take 75% of listed time before Anger effects. |
| Gadianton manual gathering | Permitted labor gathers at 45% of the ordinary rate. The faction still cannot build its own farms or productive flocks or train traders; its raid and scouting income rules remain separate. |

The 100× Nephite HP curve, Lamanite 65%/60% recruitment, and Gadianton 35% gathering rate on the v0.8 pages describe the v0.8 baseline and its validation. They are not active v0.9 values. The cheaper Nephite recruitment trial was rejected; normal 1.25×/1.20× multipliers remain. Further raid-policy and opening-position comparisons are under way; these rules are retained source behavior, not a declaration that competitive balance is finished. Death batching and the second-pass recruitment discount are diagnostics behind explicit opt-in paths, not normal-match defaults.
