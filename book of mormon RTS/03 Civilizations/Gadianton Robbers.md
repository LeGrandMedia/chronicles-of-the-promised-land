# Gadianton Robbers

**Stealth and an economy of plunder:** scout economies, steal cargo, retreat from detection and deliver it. Defended sites, interrupted raids and exposed lodges undermine income.

- [[Gadianton Plunder and Stealth]] owns Storehouse-set raid routes, laborer-cargo theft, Lasciviousness thresholds, deceptive conversion, Hideout garrisoning and concealed HQs/Encampments.
- **Conspiring Civilians** gather Timber/Precious Things and wild/foreign Food at 35% of ordinary rate; no own farms/productive flocks or traders. [[Resource System]].
- The military **Band of Kishkumen** retains ID `gadianton_robber`; faction/unit names must remain distinct.
- Secret Combination Lodges train Lawyers and generate Lasciviousness until scouted. Lawyer conversion is **Bribe**; the Lawyer is revealed from stealth while channeling, while converted units retain their deceptive former-owner appearance. Judgment Seats are retired; towers counter infiltration.
- Stable roster, research, renamed buildings and age-choice heroes: [[Data and Assets]], [[Upgrade Catalog]]. No blanket HP or recruitment modifier.

Inspiration: Helaman 2; 6; 3 Nephi 4. Roles: [[Units]], [[Structures]]; [[Voice Lines]]. Adaptation boundaries: [[Design Contract]].
