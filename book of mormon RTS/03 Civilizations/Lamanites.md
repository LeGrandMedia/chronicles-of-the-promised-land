# Lamanites

**Mass armies and Zoramite elites:** cheap rapid recruitment gains further momentum from Anger. Ordinary unit HP and defended conversion threats provide counterplay.

- [[Combat and Training Rules]] owns recruitment/age rules. [[Favor of the Lord]] owns Anger's gathering, attack and training effects and Wrath of the Fallen.
- Workers are **Idolatrous Laborers**; religious units are **Priestcrafters**. Their building is **Rameumptom from the outset**, superseding the earlier Great and Spacious Building name; its stable ID remains `temple_of_the_land`.
- Order of the Nehors adds Priestcrafter Precious Things; By the Pride of Their Hearts enables defensive conversion. Stiffen the Neck creates Zoramite Captains, generating Anger and supporting military attack speed. A Priestcrafter outside Rameumptom gives allied military +5% attack in 6m; one Priestcrafter may occupy it for +15% in 8m. The visible aura follows its current affected area. Lofty Rameumptom triples sight/conversion range. [[Conversion and Ammonites]], [[Upgrade Catalog]].
- No Watchtowers. Use current wicked-themed age heroes; Lamoni/Samuel are retired faction choices. Three choices per advancement; full roster/perks in [[Data and Assets]].

Inspiration: Alma 43:4–6; Alma 31. Roles: [[Units]], [[Structures]]; [[Voice Lines]]. Gameplay bonuses are adaptations under [[Design Contract]].
