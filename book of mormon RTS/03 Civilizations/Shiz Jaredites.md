# Shiz Jaredites

**Relentless offense:** pin opponents with aggressive infantry while beasts establish durable forward structures. Reduced infantry resilience punishes prolonged ranged exposure.

- Nonhero combat infantry has +15% attack and −10% base HP; apply HP change before research. Ordinary recruitment and gathering otherwise.
- Shares beast/Cement economy, durable architecture, locked-tier foundations, any-beast completed repairs and Oath Keeper/Glowing Stones mechanics with [[Coriantumr Jaredites]]: [[Jaredite Cement Construction]], [[Favor of the Lord]], [[Conversion and Ammonites]].
- Distinct Jaredite Shield Bearer/Warrior/Archer roles replace copied Nephite/Lamanite infantry. Hero choices alternate beast-income/build-speed perks; Shiz identity remains offensive even though structures are cement.
- Exact roster, age-choice heroes, faction labels, values and research: [[Data and Assets]], [[Upgrade Catalog]]. Do not restore the old evolution roster.

Inspiration: Ether 14–15; mechanics are adaptations under [[Design Contract]]. Roles: [[Units]], [[Structures]]; [[Voice Lines]].
