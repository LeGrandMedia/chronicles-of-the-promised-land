# Coriantumr Jaredites

**Industrial fortress:** protect builder beasts and expensive infrastructure, develop passive livestock income and establish durable defenses. Builder losses and concentration create counterplay.

- Precious Things gathering is 1.15× standard; no blanket infantry/recruitment modifier.
- Beast tiers, separate Cement resource, age gates, healing, flock/Elephant limits, locked-tier foundations and any-beast completed repairs: [[Jaredite Cement Construction]].
- Oath Keepers convert and generate **Glowing Stones**, improving building HP. The same-named barge-vision research is separate. [[Favor of the Lord]], [[Conversion and Ammonites]].
- Use distinct Jaredite Shield Bearer/Warrior/Archer roles rather than copied Nephite/Lamanite infantry. Hero choices alternate beast-income/build-speed perks; current rosters, labels and values: [[Data and Assets]], [[Upgrade Catalog]].

Inspiration: Ether 9–10; 15. Cement imagery adapts Helaman 3; not a scriptural Jaredite attribution. Roles: [[Units]], [[Structures]]; [[Voice Lines]]. See [[Design Contract]].
