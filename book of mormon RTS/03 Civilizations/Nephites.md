# Nephites

**Elite covenant army:** expensive losses and religious strength create large swings in resilience. Protect food/Temple infrastructure and convert exposed troops into nonviolent support.

- [[Combat and Training Rules]] owns recruitment modifiers and hero progression. [[Favor of the Lord]] defines the nonlinear Favor-to-HP curve, validated in v0.8; see [[Validation Report]].
- Teachers use innate Soften the Heart. Ammonites support healing/economy; Mighty Change grants Prostrate. Stripling Warriors remain separately trained, with Their Mothers Knew rather than a conversion/kill prerequisite. [[Conversion and Ammonites]].
- Distinct age choices combine military/economic/religious perks; three options per advancement. Current hero availability, prices and rosters: [[Data and Assets]], [[Upgrade Catalog]].
- Dwellings and Nephite structures use their faction labels; walls require research. No additional blanket attack bonus.

Inspiration: Alma 43; 24; 56–57. Specific stats/rosters are adaptations under [[Design Contract]]. Roles: [[Units]], [[Structures]]; voiced coverage: [[Voice Lines]].
