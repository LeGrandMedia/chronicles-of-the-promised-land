# House of Laban

**Wealthy compact guard state:** build a Precious Things economy, protect elite guards and support laborers with Concubines. Limited map coverage punishes overextension.

- Precious Things gathering 1.20×; ordinary recruitment and no blanket armor bonus. **One of the Fifty** is limited to 50 alive/queued.
- **Henpecked Gentiles** work **Vineyards**. The **Winery** is Laban's primary religious building, trains Drunken Concubines and generates 0.5 Drunkenness/s with no Food. Age-III Winery Abundance adds 3 Food/s; Age-III Fermented Reserves adds 20% Winery Drunkenness. HQ is **Laban's Crib**; the separate **Great and Spacious Building** unlocks at Age IV, is limited to one and generates five times the Winery's Drunkenness. [[Resource System]], [[Structures]].
- Drunken Concubines weaken enemies and improve nearby laborer gathering/construction by 20%; each aura radius is shown. **Seduce** creates ordinary Laban workers. The Great and Spacious Building has an 8m weakening aura. Drunkenness grants guards melee splash. [[Favor of the Lord]], [[Conversion and Ammonites]].
- Chief Captains and King's Chariots unlock in Age III; One of the Fifty in Age IV. Gate Segment research is available to Laban. Laban buildings have distinct, far-zoom-readable silhouettes; homes are smaller than the Seat of the People, and the Great and Spacious Building is largest. Chariot transport and age-choice heroes follow shared rules; King Noah boosts religious generation. Current roster/labels/perks: [[Data and Assets]], [[Upgrade Catalog]].

Inspiration: 1 Nephi 3–4. Expanding a household into a full civilization and borrowing other narrative characters are deliberate adaptations under [[Design Contract]]. Roles: [[Units]], [[Structures]]; [[Voice Lines]].
