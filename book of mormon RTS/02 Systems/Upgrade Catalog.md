- One training/research queue per producer; research once per player, never duplicated across queues. Require completed owned prerequisites/listed age. Recruitment modifiers never affect research, structures or age choices. Refunds: [[Combat and Training Rules]].
- Permanent effects apply to existing/future eligible units; HP preserves health fraction. Add applicable economic bonuses; strongest identical auras only. Hide later tiers until prerequisites researched; show faction-relevant effects.

Nephites/Lamanites have three choices each advancement, others two. Current choice effects are data; King Noah boosts religious generation.
