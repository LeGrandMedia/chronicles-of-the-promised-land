# Jaredite Cement Construction

Both [[Coriantumr Jaredites]] and [[Shiz Jaredites]] use beast-built durable cement architecture. Laborers gather but cannot construct/repair. Ships are units. Costs, gates and defense stats: [[Data and Assets]].

## Independent livestock

Retire the Cumelom → Curelom → Elephant evolution chain/husbandry unlocks. Train distinct economic beasts, all zero population and excluded from idle-worker selection:

| Beast | Unlock / production | Construction | Passive income |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Curelom | Age I; HQ/flock access per catalog | Ages I–II | Timber |
| Cumelom | Age III; Gathering of Flocks | Ages I–III | Precious Things |
| Elephant | Age IV; Gathering of Flocks | All, including special Cement defenses | Cement |

Friendly completed flocks heal nearby beasts. Each supports ten queued/living beasts; one Elephant per flock. Jaredites may build five flocks, increased to seven by Age-IV **Enlarge the Flocks**. Tiered Storehouse research/age choices improve animal income/build speed. Surviving HQs train Cureloms to recover from total builder loss; no worker exception or free spawn.

## Construction

Elephants generate separate spendable **Cement**, superseding the three-resource-only restriction. Special Cement defenses use it alongside listed materials; ordinary architecture retains cement visual identity.

Curelom/Cumelom/Elephant construction rates are 1×/1.5×/2× with locked building-HP multipliers 3×/4×/5×. Add wall research before multiplying by builder tier and Glowing Stones. Foundation HP scales with completed work, preserving damage fraction; completion never heals. Refund policy: [[Combat and Training Rules]].

Nearby eligible beasts advance construction without a persistent work order. All queued foundations/full wall chains appear immediately; beasts advance several nearby segments simultaneously. This replaces the one-designated-builder restriction. Upgrade individual completed segments to gates for team passage. Completed cement structures can be repaired by any Jaredite builder beast, regardless of tier; repair does not change the structure's locked construction tier or HP multiplier. Repairs pay proportional Timber for actual HP, preserving fractional accounting.

Cement Towers use **Glowing Stone Trebuchet**: range equals LOS, splash, minimum range and reduced building damage; exact values live with current data/Balance-v0.7. The **Glowing Stones** religious stat boosts building HP only; separate same-named research retains barge vision.

## Current tier rules and source boundary

Foundation work is locked to its original builder tier and eligible tier; lower-tier beasts cannot resume it. Any Jaredite beast may repair a completed cement structure without changing its tier or HP multiplier. These v0.8 rules passed native validation; see [[Validation Report]]. Proximity construction still allows several nearby valid builders to assist.

Ether 9:19 supplies useful animals, not evolution/building roles. User spelling **Cumelom** is retained (scriptural word: cumom). Helaman 3:7–11 supplies cement imagery, not Jaredite attribution. Mechanics are adaptations.
