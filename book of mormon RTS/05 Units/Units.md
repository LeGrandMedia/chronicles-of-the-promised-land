# Units

Current base costs, ages, faction availability, and ordinary statistics: [[Data and Assets]]. Ability requirements and exact numbers are recorded here when they are not simple catalog stats. Religious units auto-target eligible enemies; right-click issues conversion with a five-second channel and no cooldown, and converted units cannot be converted again. Shared combat, stealth, construction, and plunder rules are in their system notes. Scripture references are centralized in [[Source Ledger]]. Historical sections preserve unique concepts; current faction whitelists govern availability, including retired heroes.

## Amalickiah

Hero. Dissension targets an enemy military building within 6m, channels for 3s, pauses its queue for 6s, and has a 45s cooldown. Amalickiah cannot convert units. Voice: [[Voice Lines#Amalickiah]].

## Ammon

Legacy hero; absent from current faction whitelists. Preserved ability: Disarm affects a nonhero within 2m, preventing attacks for 4s; cooldown 15s. Each distinct target grants +2 attack, up to +20. Sling alternate attack: 12 damage every 1.5s at 6m. Voice: [[Voice Lines#Ammon]].

## Ammonite Covenant Keeper

Conversion-only support; a successful conversion of an eligible enemy creates one. Cannot be trained or selected as an age-up hero. Heals one ally at 12 HP/s within 5m; healing does not stack on a target. Gathers at 1.5× normal Nephite rate and cannot attack. After Mighty Change of Heart, an Ammonite can activate Prostrate: for 5s, any unit that kills it converts into a Covenant Keeper; cooldown 12s. Voice: [[Voice Lines#Ammonite Covenant Keeper]].

## Bowman

Ranged unit with longer range than a Slinger of Stones, but weaker in melee. Voice: [[Voice Lines#Bowman]].

## Captain Moroni

Hero. Title of Liberty plants a banner within 4m; for 30s, allies within 8m gain +15% attack. Cooldown 60s. Does not stack with the Chief Captain attack aura. Voice: [[Voice Lines#Captain Moroni]].

## Chief Captain

Age III support-combat unit. Nearby allied combat units gain +10% attack within 8m; only the strongest such aura applies. After Fight Like Dragons, the Lamanite Chief Captain grants +20% melee attack within 8m for 10s, with a 60s cooldown; strongest attack aura only. It can also train at the Rameumptom. The separate conversion-only **Zoramite Captain** (`zoramite_captain`) generates 0.12 Anger/s and grants nearby allied military +15% attack speed within 6m; it is not the trained Chief Captain. Voice: [[Voice Lines#Chief Captain]].

## Cimeter Bearer

Fast flanker; deals +50% damage to buildings. Voice: [[Voice Lines#Cimeter Bearer]].

## Coriantumr

Hero. Last Man Standing grants +2 attack for 20s whenever an allied combat unit dies within 10m. Stacks expire individually; maximum bonus +20. Voice: [[Voice Lines#Coriantumr]].

## Corrupt Judge

Retired infiltrator concept, retained for identity and legacy references after the Judgment Seat was removed. Injunction channels within 6m to reduce an economic building's throughput by 25%; at most one Judge can affect each building. Can divert output using [[Gadianton Plunder and Stealth]], with 80-resource capacity. Cannot attack directly. Voice: [[Voice Lines#Corrupt Judge]].

## Cumelom

Separate Jaredite livestock unit, not an evolution. Age III; trained from a Gathering of Flocks. The earlier HQ/husbandry acquisition route was superseded by independent livestock. Produces 0.3 Precious Things/s, costs no population, and builds Age I–III structures. Voice: [[Voice Lines#Cumelom]].

## Curelom

Separate Age I Jaredite livestock unit, not an evolution. Trained from the Seat of the People; produces 0.4 Timber/s, costs no population, and builds Age I–II structures. Voice: [[Voice Lines#Curelom]].

## Drunken Concubine

Age II religious unit trained at the Winery. **Seduce** converts an eligible enemy into a Henpecked Gentile. The 5m aura reduces enemy movement speed 20%, attack speed 25%, and maximum HP 15%, preserving current HP fraction. It increases nearby Laban laborer gathering and construction speed by 20%; the affected aura radius is visible. Laban's age-choice hero can extend the aura radius to 7m. See [[Conversion and Ammonites]] and [[Favor of the Lord]].

## Elephant

Separate Age IV Jaredite livestock unit, not an evolution. Trained from a Gathering of Flocks; produces 0.8 Cement/s, costs no population, builds in every age, and is limited to one per flock. Elephants can serve as siege and transport, garrison units, and build Jaredite cement defenses; transport capacity is 10 population. Voice: [[Voice Lines#Elephant]].

## Gadianton Robber

Band of Kishkumen (stable catalog ID: `gadianton_robber`). Fast raider trained from Encampment or Storehouse; 80-resource cargo and stealth after 3s idle. Steals laborer cargo before dropoff. Attacks reveal it for 5s. Storehouse raid routes, garrisoning and Lasciviousness thresholds are defined in [[Gadianton Plunder and Stealth]]. Selection voice: “I loooooove precious things” (user-authored original dialogue). Voice: [[Voice Lines#Gadianton Robber]].

## Gadianton

Hero. Secret Combination gives allied plunder carriers within 10m +40 capacity for 20s; cooldown 60s. Excess cargo remains after the effect, but carriers cannot load more above their normal capacity. Voice: [[Voice Lines#Gadianton]].

## Giddianhi

Hero. Demand Surrender reduces an enemy resource building's throughput by 20% for 15s; target within 8m, cooldown 60s. Only the strongest slowdown applies. Voice: [[Voice Lines#Giddianhi]].

## Hagoth's Vessel

Transport carrying 10 population; no attack. If destroyed, passengers drown unless within 5m of walkable shore. Warn before boarding. Voice: [[Voice Lines#Hagoth's Vessel]].

## Helaman

Legacy hero; absent from current faction whitelists. Preserved ability: Firm and Undaunted gives allied Stripling Warriors within 8m +2 armor for 15s; cooldown 45s. Voice: [[Voice Lines#Helaman]].

## Industrious Laborer

Worker who can gather resources and switch jobs freely. Non-Jaredites use Laborers to build; Jaredite laborers cannot build or repair cement structures. Faction display names share this worker identity: Lamanite Idolatrous Laborer, House of Laban Henpecked Gentile, and Gadianton Conspiring Civilian. Gadianton workers can gather Timber, Precious Things, wild food, and food from other players' completed farms/flocks, including allies; they cannot build their own farms/flocks. Units converted by Gadianton Lawyers appear to their former owner as still theirs; the former owner cannot command them and can only remove them manually. Signs and Symbols makes enemy workers drop resources at the nearer Gadianton Storehouse, where the deposit belongs to Gadianton. Voice: [[Voice Lines#Industrious Laborer]].

## Jaredite Barge

Jaredite transport carrying 12 population; it is not a cement structure and is trained from the dock. Glowing Stones raises its vision to 14m. Passengers drown if it is destroyed more than 5m from walkable shore. Voice: [[Voice Lines#Jaredite Barge]].

## Keeper of Flocks

Laborer job assigned to a Gathering of Flocks slot, not a separately trained unit; no extra training cost. Voice: [[Voice Lines#Keeper of Flocks]].

## King's Chariot

Age III transport in horse form. Multiple units can board by right-click; press E to unload. Capacity is 6 population. Garrisoned units share vision and cannot use abilities; if the chariot is destroyed they exit nearby at their current health. It does not attack. Voice: [[Voice Lines#King's Chariot]].

## Lawyer

Gadianton religious unit. Its conversion action is **Bribe**; starting the channel reveals the Lawyer from stealth. Successfully converted units retain their former-owner appearance, while their true owner controls them. The Secret Combination Lodge generates Lasciviousness while unscouted. See [[Gadianton Plunder and Stealth]] and [[Conversion and Ammonites]].

## Kishkumen

Hero. Assassination adds 60 damage against a hero or Judge within 2m; cooldown 45s. Does not bypass armor or Favor. Enters stealth after 3s idle. Voice: [[Voice Lines#Kishkumen]].

## Laban

Hero. Sword of Laban grants +8 passive attack, already included in displayed attack. The v0.5 support change replaced his One of the Fifty armor bonus with Concubine support: within 8m, allied Concubines gain +1 armor and their aura radius grows from 5m to 7m. Former +10% global/+25% nearby Seduction-speed bonuses are superseded by v0.7's fixed five-second conversion; runtime uses those 1.10×/1.25× factors for Concubine laborer-aura strength instead. Voice: [[Voice Lines#Laban]].

## Lamoni

Legacy hero; absent from current faction whitelists. Preserved ability: Royal Muster makes owned Encampments within 12m train 20% faster for 20s; cooldown 60s. Applies after the faction training modifier. Voice: [[Voice Lines#Lamoni]].

## Man of Moroni

Heavy defender that holds gates and deals +100% damage to buildings. Voice: [[Voice Lines#Man of Moroni]].

## Man of the Sword

Baseline melee unit that deals +50% damage to buildings, providing basic siege capability. Voice: [[Voice Lines#Man of the Sword]].

## One of the Fifty

Age IV guard infantry; maximum 50 living or queued. Deals +100% damage to buildings. For House of Laban, raw splash attack is `attack × 0.35 × Drunkenness / 100`, before each target's armor/modifiers, with radius `1.4m + 0.015m × Drunkenness`; thus 35% splash applies at 100 Drunkenness. Voice: [[Voice Lines#One of the Fifty]].

## Rameumptom Priest

Legacy support-priest concept retained for reference; no current unit uses this ID. The current **Priestcrafter** (`priestcrafter`) generates 0.06 Anger/s and uses Stiffen the Neck to create Zoramite Captains. One Priestcrafter may occupy a Lamanite Temple: occupied Priests grant +15% allied military attack in an 8m aura; unoccupied Priests grant +5% in 6m. The currently affected radius is shown on the map. Voice: [[Voice Lines#Rameumptom Priest]].

## Samuel the Lamanite

Legacy hero; absent from current faction whitelists. Preserved ability: Wall Sermon channels from a walkable wall platform and grants +2 armor to allies within 10m. Cannot attack and refuses an attack order. Voice: [[Voice Lines#Samuel the Lamanite]].

## Secret Murderer

Legacy assassin, absent from current faction whitelists. Enters stealth after 3s without attacking. First hit from stealth adds 30 damage; cooldown 20s. Can Plunder, with 40-resource capacity. Voice: [[Voice Lines#Secret Murderer]].

## Shiz

Hero. Not Quite Dead triggers once per life: the first fatal blow leaves Shiz at 1 HP for 2s, allowing one final basic attack; then he dies regardless of healing. Voice: [[Voice Lines#Shiz]].

## Slinger of Stones

Low-cost ranged support; the projectile target must be visible when fired. Voice: [[Voice Lines#Slinger of Stones]].

## Stripling Warrior

Maximum 20 living or queued. It is distinct from conversion-only Ammonites. Once per life at Favor 75 or higher, a fatal blow leaves the warrior at 1 HP and suppresses attacks for 5s. Voice: [[Voice Lines#Stripling Warrior]].

## Teacher of the Word

Nephite religious unit. Soften the Heart automatically converts eligible nearby enemies without research. The legacy non-Nephite Teacher role heals allies at 4 HP/s within 5m; current faction whitelists use other religious identities. Has no attack; a Nephite attack order or right-click on an eligible enemy means convert. Lamanite priests are called Priestcrafters, not Teachers of the Word. See [[Conversion and Ammonites]]. Voice: [[Voice Lines#Teacher of the Word]].

## Teancum

Age IV hero. Night Raid grants stealth for 10s and +40 first-hit damage, then ends; cooldown 45s. Towers and spies detect him normally. His age-choice train-speed perk is separate. Voice: [[Voice Lines#Teancum]].

## Tiller of the Earth

Existing Laborer job assigned to a Fields of Grain slot; not a separate purchase. Retains entity ID, health, equipment, and population. Voice: [[Voice Lines#Tiller of the Earth]].

## Trader in Precious Things

Player-set Galavant route. Each own worker within 4m yields 2 Precious Things, once every 30s per owner. The shared cooldown prevents overlapping traders from paying twice for the same visit. Cannot attack; an attack order becomes evade. Voice: [[Voice Lines#Trader in Precious Things]].

## Wilderness Spy

Scout detects and reveals ambushes within 6m. Gadianton version gains stealth after 3s idle and out of combat. Voice: [[Voice Lines#Wilderness Spy]].

## Worker in Ore

Existing Laborer job assigned to a Hill of Ore slot; not a separate purchase and has no extra training cost. Voice: [[Voice Lines#Worker in Ore]].
